# Assumptions

- For the explicit 2026-10-07 build date, last month is September 2026, the last completed UTC calendar month. Historical June 2020 remains a distinct source period.
- The eventual REST request uses en.wikipedia, all-access and user. Those are intended filters, not proven filters of the historical CSV.
- Household-name curation is a documented editorial judgment, not measured player recognition. The research candidate list includes 83 titles and yields 60 distinct selected titles.
- Inclusive ratios are 1.2–3. Prefer pairs closest to 1.6, then lexical title ties, with no title reuse in the sample. This is a deterministic greedy selection, not a claim of optimal maximum matching.
- Counts and their sums must be nonnegative safe integers; incomplete months, duplicate dates and unsafe sums reject rather than becoming misleading metrics.
- Historical counts are published CC0 numerical observations. Current API validity, an independent source for facts and 30 manual second-source checks remain outstanding.
- No unsupported fact line is invented. The historical sample is explicitly incomplete and currentMonthVerified is false.
- Use the existing isolated checkout; preserve concurrent claims and do not create a worktree unless explicitly requested.
