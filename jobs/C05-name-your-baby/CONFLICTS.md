# Source conflicts and choices

A prior Random(17) sample of 30 entries from the 500 historical candidates compared
two independently retrieved SSA-derived publications. All 30 peak decades agree;
only 9 peak counts agree. Twenty-one count disagreements remain unresolved.
The comparison is automated, uses the same underlying publisher, and overlaps
the first 30-name technical sample in only one name. It is not 30 manual checks
of this sample and not independent corroboration of a short fact.

Use the pinned hadley/babynames snapshot for every reported historical value;
do not merge revised counts across snapshots. Its package declares CC0. The
cause of differences is unverified; archive/tabulation revisions may contribute,
but no specific cause is asserted without source evidence. Re-check original
SSA data and explain any chosen snapshot before publishing the full pack.


The current official 2026 archive revises annual records from the 2021 mirror.
For the selected current 500 names, 21,576 common annual keys changed, two were
added and eight disappeared. All complete-period peak decades agree, but only
47 complete-period peak counts agree. Use the current official ZIP snapshot for
all current candidate metrics; preserve historical mirrors only as separately
labeled fixtures. Do not combine counts from different tabulation dates.
The live March 2026 decade tables agreed with all 30 randomly selected current
peak fields in the documented manual review. This resolves the chosen current
count source; it does not explain every underlying SSA record revision.

Including observed 2020–2025 counts changes both volume eligibility and peak
clarity. Eighteen categories lose the 15% clarity margin when those subtotals
are included; most newly eligible names cross the 50,000 total threshold.
The final selection has five partial-period winners. Compare raw observed
published subtotals and label the incomplete period rather than projecting or
normalizing the partial decade. A future build must select anew from its actual
archive and cannot silently reuse this snapshot's eligibility or provenance.

Name facts with uncertain or disputed etymologies are held for review. Merely
finding a dictionary headword or a Wikipedia article is not corroboration.
Wikipedia text that derives its only support from Behind the Name is not a
second independent authored source for that claim. Specific disagreements and
chosen safer paraphrases will be recorded here as facts are accepted.
