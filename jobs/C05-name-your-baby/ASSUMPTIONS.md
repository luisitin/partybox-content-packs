# Assumptions

- The historical sample compares thirteen complete decades, 1880–2009, from a mirror covering 1880–2017. The partial 2010s are excluded. Each sample peak is restricted to its declared window, not an all-time or current peak. Current official data now covers 1880–2025 and is being audited for the full pack.
- Counts are published SSA occurrences for exact spelling and one recorded F/M category. Retain categories without inferring an individual's gender identity or merging categories.
- A missing annual row means unknown or unpublished count, potentially privacy suppression. A decade sum is a published subtotal and never proves zero births. Sparkline cells record published and omitted years.
- Usage does not prove broad recognition. At least 50,000 published occurrences and a peak at least 115% of its runner-up are engineering candidate filters requiring editorial review.
- Sort qualifying categories by total published count, ASCII name and category. Retain the first qualifying category per exact name; it need not be globally dominant among categories that fail the peak filter.
- Earliest decade wins peak ties. Equal peaks fail the clarity filter. Require safe integer sums and exact integer ratio comparisons without Infinity values.
- SSA-derived mirrors are distinct retrieval routes, not independent original fact publishers. Automated comparisons do not replace manual checks.
- Sample facts remain null and unverified. Two historical reference candidates are recorded for later review; they do not establish full coverage or current scholarly accuracy.
- Build-time Python reads the pinned RDA. Strict ES2022 TypeScript game logic has no runtime dependencies, network calls, clocks or implicit randomness.
- Use the existing isolated checkout, preserve concurrent files/claims, and create no worktree unless explicitly requested.
- The official 2026 source has observed years 1880–2025. Its 2020s subtotal covers six observed years; 2026–2029 are unobserved, not suppressed or zero. Full-pack peaks compare observed published subtotals without projections or annual-density normalization. Every chart must label the partial period. Missing published rows within observed years are recorded separately.
- The current official archive revises older published counts. Historical mirror comparisons may corroborate patterns but cannot verify current totals or serve as a second independent authored fact source.
- Editorial curation is a separate source input. A reviewed fact needs two authored works, explicit reviewer evidence and at most 90 Unicode characters. Distinct URLs alone do not prove independent authorship. Recognition has its own review gate and cannot be inferred from a reviewed fact.
- The first reviewed subset uses 100 medieval dictionary pairs and 44 additional historical-book pairs, each compared with the separately authored Behind the Name dictionary. Eight overlaps retain the medieval pair. Quotation whitespace is collapsed without changing words. Earlier disputed meanings are narrowed or held rather than silently harmonized.
- Copyrighted reference prose is not an open dataset. Retain only brief supporting quotations and original factual paraphrases, with attribution; do not redistribute the dictionaries or scraped descriptions. No open-license claim is made for those expressive excerpts.
- Recognition review is an explicit editorial judgment for adult US general-audience players, not a measured survey. The separate reviewer accepted 487 exact forms and held 13 uncertain forms. Its notes are carried in curation; a false flag does not assert that a name is unknown. Complete remains false while any recognition decision or fact is unresolved.
- Expanded fact pairs can use pinned Wikipedia editorial entries when the relevant clause has a non-Behind-the-Name citation scope actually reviewed. The referenced underlying books are not claimed to have been directly read. The named Wikipedia work, not its unread cited book, is the second authored source. Directly read book/dictionary alternatives are preferred.
- The final current selection is an explicit fixed 500-ID manifest rather than the top 500 by counts alone. Preserve the 487 accepted exact forms and add 13 familiar source-qualified reserves. Rank the selected set by published total, ASCII name and category; all still meet the unchanged usage/peak requirements. The 13 replaced holds are not asserted to be invalid SSA spellings.
- All 500 selected names now have explicit editorial recognition notes. This records review completion, not measured human familiarity. Missing independent facts still prevent complete-pack delivery. The name-related fact can concern a different historical bearer/category without claiming that every SSA family shares that person's ancestry or name origin.

- Modern variant linkage supported only by one dictionary stays editorial context. Player facts name the root/bearer independently described by two works; older books are not claimed to contain absent modern spellings. Generic review limits are centralized here and in SOURCES; review notes remain within the strict1000-character gate.
