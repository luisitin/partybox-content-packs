This is a metadata engineering fallback, not a completed museum-image pack.

not associated with any museum object and establish no photograph license.
All fact lines remain null and unverified.

Thirty source record URLs and required hashes are stored in
fixtures/source.json and the source manifest. The selected records are
24 Cooper Hewitt objects from commit 4272b8fa73697845507ff40cafeb19310218c896
(2017) and six AIC objects from 8936f25879fd688fc2412436e5df4e30402bd081
(2025). Only factual titles, original date labels and structured bounds are
selected; source descriptions and photo pixels are omitted. Twenty-four
numeric ranges remain null exactly as in the source.

An ancillary Grace Hopper photo and Smithsonian HDR landscape were researched
but are unpaired to these museum objects. Neither is redistributed or counted
toward object-image completeness. A third-party declaration is not original
license verification.
