# Conflicts and choices

- Cooper Hewitt releases CC0 metadata and explicitly excludes images. Keep image URLs as references only and never apply metadata permission to the pixels.
- Six AIC records provide structured bounds, while 24 Cooper Hewitt records only provide text dates. Keep the latter's bounds/centuries null rather than inventing an interval.
- AIC gives descriptions separate CC-BY/Terms treatment. Omit descriptions and use only its declared CC0 factual metadata.
- An unrelated Grace Hopper photo has a third-party public-domain declaration, and a Smithsonian HDR landscape is CC0. Neither is paired to the 30 museum objects; neither counts toward the required photographs or is redistributed here.
- Source snapshots are 2017 and 2025. They do not establish current media license pages or a second independent factual publisher.
