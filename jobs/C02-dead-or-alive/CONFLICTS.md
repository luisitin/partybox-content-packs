  historical fixture selector excludes that row rather than inventing a correction.
- Missing death dates in the historical snapshot disagree with the inference
  that someone is alive today. Preserve missing evidence and return no answer.

  has no 60-sitelink or independently verified current-status evidence; its
  explicitly AI-generated avatars were not used as Commons portraits.

  licenses. No paid content, source motivation quotes or unverified portrait
